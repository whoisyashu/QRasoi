# Project Overview

## Product Name

QRasoi

---

## Tagline

Simple Digital Menus & Order Management for Every Restaurant.

---

## Vision

Build the easiest restaurant ordering platform for small and medium restaurants in India.

Restaurant owners should be able to create their account, upload their menu, download a QR code and start accepting digital orders within ten minutes.

The product should require almost zero training.

## Problem Statement

Most small restaurants still rely on printed menus.

Printed menus become outdated whenever prices change.

Customers waste time waiting for menus.

Restaurant staff manually write orders.

Communication between customer, owner and chef is inefficient.

QRasoi solves this using a digital menu and simple order workflow.

---

## Core Product

Customer scans QR.

Customer browses menu.

Customer places order.

Customer receives Order ID.

Restaurant verifies payment.

Restaurant confirms order.

Chef prepares order.

Customer tracks live status.

Customer collects food.

---

## Product Objectives

Reduce ordering time.
