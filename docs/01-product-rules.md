# Product Rules

These rules are non-negotiable.

Every frontend implementation must follow them.

---

RULE 1

Customer does not create an account.

---

RULE 2

Customer does not login.

---

RULE 3

Restaurant creates an account.

---

RULE 4

Each restaurant receives exactly one QR Code.

---

RULE 5

QR always opens the public menu.

---

RULE 6

No table specific QR Codes.

---

RULE 7

Customer can browse without authentication.

---

RULE 8

Customer can add items to cart.

---

RULE 9

Customer can edit cart before placing order.

---

RULE 10

After placing order,

customer cannot modify it.

---

RULE 11

System generates a unique Order ID.

---

RULE 12

Customer is redirected to Live Order Status page.

---

RULE 13

Customer must visit counter.

---

RULE 14

Customer pays physically.

Cash or UPI handled outside QRasoi.

---

RULE 15

QRasoi does not process payments.

---

RULE 16

Restaurant owner verifies payment manually.

---

RULE 17

Only after payment verification,

order reaches chef.

---

RULE 18

Chef cannot see unpaid orders.

---

RULE 19

Chef can only change order status.

Admin cannot modify restaurant menus.

RULE 26

Every pending order expires automatically after configurable timeout.

---

RULE 27

Live status should update automatically.

RULE 29

Restaurant menu is always public.
